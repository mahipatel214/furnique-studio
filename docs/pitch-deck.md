# Furnique Studio — pitch deck outline

**Audience:** classroom / early-stage concept review  
**All market, customer, pricing, and funding numbers are hypotheses unless explicitly identified as test results.**

---

## 1 — Furnique Studio

**Thoughtful furniture for everyday homes.**  
A curated, room-first furniture shopping concept with clearer details and a calmer online experience.

## 2 — The customer problem

- Furniture discovery is spread across sellers and channels.
- Dimensions, materials, delivery conditions, and after-sales terms can be hard to compare.
- Shoppers need to coordinate pieces for a room, not just browse isolated products.

These are problem hypotheses to validate with target customers, not claimed research results.

## 3 — Who we serve

- Primary hypothesis: renters and first-time homeowners furnishing one room at a time in Indian cities.
- Secondary hypothesis: independent interior stylists who need a small, reliable catalogue.
- Next step: conduct 8–12 consented interviews and record anonymized findings.

## 4 — Our approach

- Curated Living, Dining, and Bedroom collections.
- Useful product, dimension, material, and care details.
- Saved items, cart, demo checkout, and order lookup.
- Future: trusted maker partnerships and coordinated delivery/assembly.

## 5 — Product demo

Show Home → category collection → product detail → cart → test checkout → order lookup.  
Show the browser admin preview only as a local mock; the protected API admin endpoint is separate. All products, reviews, stock, and payments are demo data.

## 6 — Business model

- Primary hypothesis: product sales at a positive contribution margin.
- Future optional services: delivery/assembly and design consultation.
- Model costs product-by-product: landed cost, shipping, packaging, payment, returns/damage, tax, support, and acquisition.

## 7 — Go-to-market experiments

- Search content and room guides.
- Opt-in newsletter and referral test.
- Disclosed creator partnerships.
- Small local pilot after supplier and delivery validation; no paid growth before unit economics are known.

## 8 — Competitive position

| Alternative | What Furnique would test |
|---|---|
| Large marketplaces | Smaller curated room collections and clearer product information |
| Local furniture shops | Online discovery combined with verified local-maker relationships |
| Social-media sellers | Structured specifications, order status, and explicit terms |

No defensible moat is established; maker access and support quality remain hypotheses.

## 9 — Operations and risk

- Verify product quality, dimensions, stock, packaging, delivery coverage, and returns with suppliers.
- Keep demo checkout in test mode; no real payment data.
- Build shared, transactional inventory before scaling.
- Obtain current privacy, consumer e-commerce, tax, and product-disclosure advice.

## 10 — Pilot budget hypothesis

**Illustrative ask: ₹5 lakh**, not committed/raised; verify with quotes.  
Samples/quality ₹1.25L · storage/delivery ₹1.50L · cloud/testing ₹0.50L · research/content ₹0.50L · returns/working capital ₹1.25L.

## 11 — Milestones

1. Customer interviews and usability test.
2. Supplier/sample validation and documented delivery terms.
3. Persistent inventory, protected admin, payment sandbox and security review.
4. Limited city pilot; track conversion, delivery damage, cancellations, contribution margin.
5. Scale only after repeatable positive service and unit economics.

## 12 — The ask

- Feedback from target shoppers, furniture makers, and mentors.
- Help validate supplier and last-mile assumptions.
- Review the MVP and its demo-only constraints.
- No live customer, revenue, investor, or cloud-deployment traction is claimed.
